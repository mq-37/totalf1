01 Vision & Interactive Game Modes

Product Scope

TRAF1 (Local F1) is a local-first Formula 1 web application that unites deep historical motorsport analytics with interactive racing, card battles, and team-management gameplay. Designed to run cleanly on localhost with zero cloud lock-in, the platform spans six tightly integrated experiences: a complete 1950–2026 Driver and Constructor Archive, printable Collector and Game Card decks exported as ReportLab PDFs, the 3D F1 Card Arena (Top Trumps GP, Track Simulator, Guess the Legend, and Higher/Lower), a Full CPU Broadcast Simulator with live telemetry and director cameras, a zero-scroll Team Principal Career, and Pit Wall: The Ladder—a persistent five-tier career rising from grassroots Karting through Formula 4, Formula 3, and Formula 2 into Formula 1, complete with a hybrid F2-to-F1 Career Fork.

02 Data Foundation & Fair Scoring

5 SQLite WAL DBs

Under the hood, TRAF1 combines five local SQLite databases running in concurrent WAL mode: f1.sqlite for season standings and team palettes, f1db.sqlite (1.13 million rows of qualifying, race results, retirements, and lap records), fia_docs.sqlite (11,919 official steward and regulatory documents), plus relational career save stores career_saves.sqlite and ladder_saves.sqlite. Rather than relying on raw career win totals that disproportionately reward dominant machinery, rating_engine.py executes a cached bulk pass across F1DB, FIA steward infringements, and Driver DNA to compute eight era-normalized, machinery-adjusted attributes—Pace, Racecraft, Consistency, Duels, Clutch, Wet Weather, Tyre Care, and Driver-Fault DNF%. Mechanical failures are strictly separated from driver-fault crashes, while qualifying and race teammate head-to-head rates distinguish pure driver skill from estimated car strength across cards, live races, and careers.

03 Procedural 3D Cars, Tracks & Weather

Three.js & Canvas 2D

Every vehicle in TRAF1 is procedurally sculpted in Three.js via f1_car_3d.js using year-faithful chassis geometry spanning 1950s cigar torpedoes, 1968 high wings, 1975 periscope airboxes, 2017 shark-fin T-wings, and 2026 compact active-aero cars. A year-aware livery registry resolves constructors onto 23 bespoke 3D pattern styles—including matte Red Bull bull-nose liveries, Ferrari HP blue-and-white accents, exposed-carbon Mercedes arrows, and classic heritage stripes—paired with matte, gloss, metallic, or carbon finishes. A headless Chrome studio renderer pre-bakes 1,231 team-year car icons deduplicated through a manifest.json alias map into 241 canonical PNGs, cutting disk usage by 80%. On race day, f1_track_renderer.js draws 79 authentic circuit layouts featuring Open-Meteo Doppler weather radar, surface-versus-core tyre cliffs, dirty-air downforce loss, pit-rejoin ghost predictors, and dynamic team radio.

04 Backend Engineering & Infra

FastAPI + Uvicorn

The server runs on Python, FastAPI, Uvicorn, and Jinja2, cleanly structured into seven domain routers (archive, cards, game, career, ladder, f1db, and dev) backed by decoupled engines for practice programmes, wind-tunnel R&D correlation, cost-cap accounting, FIA regulation voting, paddock politics, and five-tier world AI. Infrastructure middleware provides GZip compression (shrinking HTML and JS payloads by over 72%), immutable versioned static caching, startup index warmups, sub-5-millisecond cached deck APIs, a live /api/health diagnostics endpoint, and a 38-test automated verification suite ensuring rock-solid reliability across every layer.

How rating_engine.py Computes Fair Attributes

1. Bulk Index Warmup (_build_f1db_and_fia_index()) Single pass at server startup (~240ms) reading f1db.sqlite (drivers, races, races_race_results) + fia_docs.sqlite (steward penalty regex Car XX - Surname).
2. Mechanical vs. Driver-Fault DNF Separation Regex classifies reasonRetired: Engine/Gearbox/Hydraulics become mech_dnf_pct (excluded from driver penalty), while Accident/Collision/Spin become driver_fault_dnf_pct.
3. Machinery-Adjusted Skill vs. Car Strength Groups teammates by (raceId, constructorId) to compute exact Quali H2H %, Race H2H % (excl. mech DNFs), and positionsGained — isolating driver_skill from car_strength_est.
